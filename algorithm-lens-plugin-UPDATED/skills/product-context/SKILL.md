# Product Context for AlgorithmLens

## What AlgorithmLens Is

AlgorithmLens is a consumer AI transparency tool. It captures a snapshot of a user's social media feed at a specific moment, processes that snapshot into analyzable categories, and renders the results into a six-tab dashboard (Overview, Sources, Ads, Politics, Tone, Suggested vs. Followed).

## What AlgorithmLens Is NOT

- Not an algorithm exposure platform
- Not an accusation engine
- Not a reverse-engineering tool
- Not an investigative journalism tool
- Not a partisan analysis tool

## The Goodish Initiative

AlgorithmLens is the flagship project under Goodish, a broader initiative focused on building tools that increase human agency in AI-mediated environments. Goodish is grounded in the belief that visibility precedes agency, and agency precedes informed engagement.

## Core Thesis

Platforms possess extremely granular internal dashboards about content distribution, engagement, and monetization. Individual users experience feeds as opaque streams. This asymmetry is both philosophically troubling and practically solvable.

AlgorithmLens does not speculate about why content appears. It documents what appears. Structured transparency can exist without speculation. Agency begins with measurable awareness. Governance principles can inform consumer software. Epistemic humility builds trust.

## Market Position

No existing consumer tool provides this structured feed composition breakdown. Academic research tools exist but are inaccessible to most users. Transparency reports are aggregated and impersonal. Journalism is episodic rather than individualized. AlgorithmLens fills the space between institutional research and personal awareness.
