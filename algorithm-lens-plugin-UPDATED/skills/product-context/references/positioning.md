# AlgorithmLens Positioning Reference

## Competitive Landscape

### Academic Tools
- Exist for researchers studying algorithmic curation
- Not accessible to everyday consumers
- Often require technical setup or institutional access
- AlgorithmLens differentiates by being consumer-facing and immediately usable

### Platform Transparency Reports
- Published by social media companies themselves
- Aggregated data, not personalized
- Published on platform's timeline, not user's
- AlgorithmLens differentiates by providing individualized, on-demand snapshots

### Journalism
- Investigative pieces about algorithmic influence
- Episodic, not continuous
- Often sensational or accusatory in tone
- AlgorithmLens differentiates by being calm, descriptive, and user-controlled

### Browser Extensions (generic)
- Some extensions track screen time or block content
- None provide structured feed composition analysis
- AlgorithmLens differentiates by analyzing what appeared, not just how long you looked

## Positioning Statement

For everyday social media users who want to understand what their feed actually contains, AlgorithmLens is a transparency tool that captures and categorizes feed content into a structured dashboard. Unlike platform-published transparency reports that show aggregate data, AlgorithmLens provides personalized, snapshot-level analysis grounded in epistemic restraint.

## Key Messaging Principles

1. **Lead with empowerment, not fear.** The product helps users understand their feeds, not frightens them about algorithms.
2. **Describe, never accuse.** All external messaging follows the same epistemic restraint as the product itself.
3. **Acknowledge limits.** The product categorizes based on observable signals and is transparent about the approximation involved.
4. **Invite curiosity.** Position the product as a lens (the name is literal) — a tool for looking more carefully at something you already experience.

## Pricing Rationale

- Free tier lowers the barrier to entry — anyone can try a scan
- Plus tier ($10/month or $96/year) is priced accessibly for individual consumers
- The premium value (longitudinal trends) is genuinely distinct from the free value (single snapshots)
- Annual pricing ($96 = $8/month effective) provides a meaningful discount to encourage commitment
- Two-week trial lets users experience trends before committing

## Beta Strategy

- Controlled beta, not open launch
- Prioritize reliability over feature breadth
- Gather structured feedback from early users
- Iterate carefully based on evidence, not pressure
- Credibility matters more than speed
