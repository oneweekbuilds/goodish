# Site diagnosis: the live AlgorithmLens pages

Date: 29 September 2026. Pages: https://www.algorithmlens.com/ (root), /explore/, /explore/specimen.html, /explore/experience-film.html, /privacy/, /terms, /methodology, as deployed from commit 3da31857. Method: Playwright with Chromium at 375, 390, 430, 768 and 1440 px, plus an iPhone 14 emulation (iPhone user agent and viewport on the Chromium engine, so Safari-only rendering is unverified). Every section, every explorer view, every dataset, the form's five outcomes, the film toggle, the details block, focus, hover and reduced motion were captured. Screenshots: `docs/web-audit/before/` (177 files). The previous audit's before set, taken of the site as it stood before the audit run, was moved to `docs/web-audit/before-2026-09-28/` so the two sets do not collide; `WEB_AUDIT.md` and `DESIGN_NOTES.md` point at the new location. The old site's brand is inventoried in `BRAND_INVENTORY.md`; the shorthand "old" below refers to it.

This is a designer's read of the screenshots, not a re-run of the audit. The audit's fixes (headers, weight, honesty, form copy, legal links) all hold and are not questioned here. Items are ranked by impact: **1** changes whether a visitor stays, **2** changes whether the site feels like a product, **3** is polish. "Fix" means it is fixed in the brand batch; "decision" means only Justin can settle it and it is listed again at the end.

## Rank 1: the first viewport does not stop a scroll

- **1.1 · fix** At 1440 the fold is a headline, a paragraph, a button and a small film. The film is 236 px wide inside a 400 px card inside a 560 px column; the copy column is 420 px tall against a 700 px card, so the copy floats in a void and the right third of the viewport is empty cream (`root-1440-fold.jpg`). Nothing moves except the small film and nothing carries the brand colors except the wordmark. The old hero filled the same two columns with a 76 px headline and a 560 px working panel that kept moving (`old/landing-1440-fold.jpg`). Fix: `1fr 560px` grid, the film in the old panel chrome (header row with a slow pulse dot and an "Example" label, the scan line, a count tally in the footer), the film at 300 px, the dot-grid paper texture behind the hero, a hollow ring eyebrow above the headline and the green hand-drawn underline under "clearly".
- **1.2 · fix** The reading order of the hero copy at 1440 is headline, italic limit, button, lede. The disclaimer comes before the promise, so the first full sentence a visitor reads says what the product cannot do. On phones the same order puts the limit between the film and the button. Fix: headline, lede, button, then the limit as the caption under the button (still in the hero, still beside the action, as the app's own surfaces do).
- **1.3 · fix** "Read the film in words" is a bare `details` summary placed in grid column 2, 60 px under the film card, with no card, no rule and no relation to the copy column (`root-1440-fold.jpg`, `root-1440-state-details-open.jpg`). It reads as a leftover. Fix: fold it into the film card's footer as the second control beside "Play the example film".
- **1.4 · fix** At 768 the page uses the phone layout: a 400 px film card centered with 184 px of cream on each side and the headline orphaned above it (`root-768-fold.jpg`, `root-768.jpg`). The breakpoint at 899 px is too high; a tablet is wide enough for two columns. Fix: two columns from 720 px, single column below.
- **1.5 · fix** No brand color in the first viewport beyond the wordmark, no texture, no motion cue. The old page's first viewport had a green pill, a blue and green headline, a pulsing dot, a moving feed and a blue/green tally. Under the canon, color is meaning, so the fix uses it where the meaning is true: the blue segment in the film tally for the one post that carried an ad label, the green underline stroke as a mark, tinted chips for the observable facts. See `BRAND_INVENTORY.md` C1 and C2.

## Rank 2: the site feels generic, flat and template-like

- **2.1 · fix** Five sections share one shape: a serif title, a paragraph at 60 ch, an optional white card, 64 px of padding and a 1 px rule (`root-1440.jpg`). Nothing on the landing is interactive except the film toggle and the embedded record. The old page varied its shapes: a working panel, a browser card, a phone you could tap, a full-bleed marquee, a selectable step list, a 2 by 2 grid, a four-card row, a worked example. Fix: reinstate those shapes with honest content (a tappable fictional feed whose readout is the record forming; the marquee of what a scan can and cannot read; the selectable three steps with a keyed preview; the privacy grid; the four "what people do with it" cards; the worked count).
- **2.2 · fix** The record section is a white card with a 720 px sandboxed frame in it. The frame shows the sheet stack floating in a white void, the finding text sits far below the stack, and the limit is stated twice: inside the frame ("More context does not by itself establish a stable rate or a certified change.") and again in the card foot ("Separate scans add context. They do not, by themselves, establish a stable rate or certify a change.") (`root-1440-s-record.jpg`). On a slow connection the frame is a blank 720 px box with no placeholder. Fix: a strip of four count tiles above the frame (posts recorded, accounts read, ad labels, scans), a sheet header with the provenance, one limit sentence, a placeholder line while the frame loads, and a 560 px frame.
- **2.3 · fix** The brand is absent between the header and the footer. Blue appears once (the wordmark) and green once (the wordmark). The old page used tinted chips, tinted icon squares and tally bars in the two brand colors on every section. Fix: use them under the color law (blue only where the fact is the platform's, green only where the fact is the reader's, charcoal for everything inferred or neutral).
- **2.4 · fix** The explorer feels like a different product from the landing. The landing is a document with a header; the explorer is an app shell with a 220 px rail, an italic rail title, a 12 px toolbar sentence, a dataset select and a file input in the top bar, an icon-only "↗" button and no primary action back to the landing's ask (`explore-1440-fold.jpg`). Its surface tint (#FFFDF9), theme color (#F7F5EF) and label style (boxed basis chip) also differ from the landing's. Fix: the explorer's top bar becomes the landing header (wordmark at the same size, "Front page", the dark "Join the launch list" pill), the rail keeps its numbered views but gains the landing's ring-label eyebrow and the same hover underline, the basis chip becomes a ring label, the tints and theme color match, the next-view button gets a visible label, and the file input moves out of the header into the rail bottom.
- **2.5 · fix** The film page frames a video of the explorer inside the explorer's own chrome, so the visitor sees a screenshot of a screenshot, and its "Example film · 24 seconds" label is a boxed chip although the rest of the site uses ring labels (`film-1440-fold.jpg`, `film-390-fold.jpg`). Fix: the landing header on the film page, a ring label, the video inside the paper card grammar with the same footer controls as the hero film.
- **2.6 · fix** Motion: the landing has no reveal, no hover on any card, no state change anywhere except the nav underline and the button color. Calm should not mean inert. Fix: the old micro-interactions at canon timings (underline sweep 240 ms, card lift 240 ms, reveal 400 ms and 12 px, pulse 2.4 s, tap ring, pop 240 ms), all off under reduced motion.
- **2.7 · fix** The wordmark is timid: 160 px wide in a 72 px header at 1440, 148 px on phones (`root-1440-fold.jpg`, `root-390-fold.jpg`). The old ran it at 38 px tall (about 190 px wide) and again large in the footer. Fix: 38 px tall in the header, 28 px in a four-column footer with the tagline beneath it.
- **2.8 · fix** The footer is one line: a tagline and five links (`root-1440.jpg`). It ends the page without weight. Fix: the old four-column footer (wordmark and tagline, Product, Company, Legal) with every current link kept exactly, and the closing row "Example data shown throughout is fictional."

## Rank 2: hierarchy and copy

- **2.9 · fix** The lede (17 px) is the same size as body text, so the promise has no more weight than the small print. Fix: 20 px lede with `text-wrap: pretty`, headlines balanced.
- **2.10 · fix** Copy that reads as filler or as instructions: "Join the launch list for news about AlgorithmLens." repeats the button; "Turn back to a date. Each sheet holds only that scan's posts." describes a sheet before the visitor has seen one; the three steps read as a manual ("Follow the app's recording instructions, then scroll a feed as you normally would."); the "Honest about limits" section is a 60 ch paragraph wall with a ring label and no shape. Fix: the launch line becomes "One email when the app is out. Nothing else."; the record lede becomes "Four dated scans, kept separate. Turn back to any of them."; the steps get the old titles' rhythm ("Start a scan", "Review what appeared", "Get a clearer picture") with one-line bodies; the limits become the can/cannot marquee with the two honesty sentences under it.
- **2.11 · fix** "The honest limit" ring label over a headline that says "Honest about limits." says the same thing twice. Fix: label "What a scan can read" over the marquee headline.
- **2.12 · decision** The hero headline. "See your feed clearly." is right and stays. The old three-color "See how the algorithms see you" cannot return (a platform-agency claim, and green text). Whether Justin wants a two-color headline at all, with blue on an honest word, is his call.

## Rank 3: spacing, rhythm, consistency

- **3.1 · fix** Section padding is a flat 64 px everywhere and every section starts with a rule, so the page has no crescendo. Fix: bands alternate cream and white, the hero and final call get 96 px, the marquee runs full bleed.
- **3.2 · fix** The launch card at 1440 puts the form's first label 8 px higher than the headline's cap height, so the two columns do not align (`root-1440-s-launch.jpg`). Fix: align the form to the headline's baseline grid.
- **3.3 · fix** The explorer's mobile chip row clips the last chip at the right edge with no fade at 375 to 768 px (`explore-768-fold.jpg`, `explore-390-fold.jpg`). Fix: a paper fade at the row's right edge and 16 px end padding.
- **3.4 · fix** The explorer's page head at 390 puts a 44 px icon-only "↗" beside a 37 px headline, and the title of the button is not visible (`explore-390-fold.jpg`). Fix: a labeled "Next view" pill under the subtitle.
- **3.5 · fix** The explorer transcript lines at 768 run about 100 characters (carried over from the audit). Fix: 62 ch measure.
- **3.6 · fix** The specimen page header says "A closer look at a feed" in 13 px muted text and has no way to the explorer except the select at the bottom (`specimen-1440-fold.jpg`). Fix: the shared header with "Front page" and "Explore the example".
- **3.7 · fix** There is no designed 404; an unknown path shows Vercel's default page. Fix: a static 404 in the paper system (the old app had a kind one, `BRAND_INVENTORY.md` 10.5).
- **3.8 · decision** `/terms` and `/methodology` are still the old app: Geist from Google Fonts, navy text, Title Case navigation, a blue callout, and a two-link header that stacks oddly at 390 ("Front page" over "Explore the example" beside the wordmark, `terms-390-fold.jpg`, `methodology-1440-fold.jpg`). They are the only pages on the site that request a third-party font. The methodology text is a locked mirror of the app's prompts and the terms carry an unresolved counsel note, so re-hosting them as static paper pages is a content decision, not a styling one.
- **3.9 · fix** The privacy page's "This website" note is a white card with a 12 px radius while every other card on the site uses 18 px; its header lacks the section nav. Fix: shared header, 18 px radius.
- **3.10 · fix** Theme color differs between pages (#F5F4F0 on the landing, #F7F5EF on the explorer and specimen), so the browser chrome shifts tint between pages on phones. Fix: one value.
- **3.11 · fix** The explorer's empty dataset shows "No posts to review." twice, once as the scene's empty state and once as the finding "Nothing to classify." (`explore-390-dataset-empty.jpg`). Acceptable, but the scene's empty ring is 64 px of nothing. Fix: the scene shows the empty sheet outline with "0 posts" so the composition holds.
- **3.12 · fix** The film's own on-screen card still says "The targeting reason is not" (audit H-7). Not fixable without a re-cut; the transcript beside it does not repeat the word. Logged, unchanged.

## What is working and must not be lost

- The italic Source Serif limit lines are the strongest voice on the site.
- "Example" or "fictional" is visible on every dataset, and counts come before rates everywhere.
- The form's five outcomes are honest and distinct.
- The explorer's views are genuinely good instruments; the diagnosis is about their chrome, not their content.
- The security headers, redirects, legal links and zero third-party requests on the static pages.
