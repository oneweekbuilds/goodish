📱 MOBILE SPECIFIC GUIDELINES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RESPONSIVE APPROACH:
  • Mobile-first design (optimize for small screens first)
  • Bottom tab bar (standard mobile UX)
  • Full-width cards with gutters (16px padding)
  • Vertical scrolling only (no horizontal scroll)

TYPOGRAPHY SCALING FOR MOBILE:
  • Hero headings: 24px (vs 32px desktop)
  • Section headers: 18px (same as desktop)
  • Body text: 14px (vs 16px desktop)
  • Small text: 12px (vs 14px desktop)
  • Labels: 10px (vs 11px desktop)

SPACING FOR MOBILE:
  • Card padding: 16px (vs 20px desktop)
  • Section gaps: 32px (vs 48px desktop)
  • Compact padding: 12px (vs 20px desktop)

ACCESSIBILITY FOR MOBILE:
  • Touch targets: 44px × 44px minimum
  • Color contrast: 4.5:1 minimum (WCAG AA)
  • Screen reader: VoiceOver (iOS), TalkBack (Android)
  • Focus states: Always visible
  • Text scaling: Must work up to 200%
