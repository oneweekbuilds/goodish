Motion, described
On first view of a scan, dots fade in once, in feed order, 12ms apart, 240ms total; bar fills draw from zero width over 420ms. Both use cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1), run once, and never replay on scroll. Details expands 280ms with content fading in over the last 120ms; the chevron rotates on the same curve. Press states tint, nothing scales or bounces.

Spec

The finding card, specified

Anatomy, top to bottom
Finding label · headline (the story, no digits) · one visual · one caption line · agency footer with Details. Details expands below the footer; nothing above it moves. Wordy counts, references and caveats live only in Details.
Type scale
Headline · system serif, New York on iOS, Charter or Georgia elsewhere23/30 · 400 · -1% Screen title, same serif26/32 · 400 Finding label · SF Pro13/18 · 500 Caption and footer prompt13/19 · 400 Details link, brand blue13/18 · 600 Chart labels and detail values · tabular12.5-13 · 600 Legend and helper text12.5/16 · 400
Color, exactly where each is used
#1868D8brand blue: the counted thing, neutral tone, Details link #20A888brand green: positive tone, source share, reference marks, all-clear states #F7F2E8cream: screen background, limits panel inside Details #FFFFFFcard surface, no shadow #23201Aink: headlines, values #6D675Cwarm grey: labels, captions, footers #ECE5D5track, the unfilled share of any bar #C7BFADempty unit ring: plain posts, zero states #E7E0D1hairlines: card border, footer and Details dividers #FFCC00 12%caution tint, sample-size pill only, never elsewhere
Spacing, radius, motion
Card padding 20, radius 12, hairline 1px. Label to headline 6, headline to visual 20, visual to legend 10, caption above 12, footer 14 above with min height 44 (full row is the tap target). Cards 12 apart, screen padding 20 by 24. Bars 24 tall radius 6, dots 12 with 6 gaps. Expand 280ms cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1); detail content fades in over the last 120ms. Press states tint the row, never scale.
Near-empty findings, like politics at zero
Keep the full card and the full geometry: all 17 units render as empty rings, so the reader sees everything was checked and nothing was found. The headline states the absence, a quiet green pill marks it as an all-clear, the caption names the sample honestly, and Details still expands. Never hide, shrink, or grey out the card.
How it generalizes to any finding
Every finding reduces to a subject count over a basis (n of N) plus an optional reference. Pick one of three visual grammars: a proportion bar when the story is how much against a benchmark, unit dots in feed order when the story is where or how often, a segmented bar when the story is a mix of 2 to 4 parts. One visual per card, subject ink alternates blue and green down the stack, everything else stays fixed. Zero results always use the empty-unit treatment above.
