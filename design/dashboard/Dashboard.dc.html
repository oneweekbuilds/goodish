Spacing, radius, motion
Card padding 20, radius 12, hairline 1px. Label to headline 6, headline to visual 20, visual to legend 10, caption above 12, footer 14 above with min height 44 (full row is the tap target). Near-empty findings, like politics at zero
Keep the full card and the full geometry: all 17 units render as empty rings, so the reader sees everything was checked and nothing was found. The headline states the absence, a quiet green pill marks it as an all-clear, the caption names the sample honestly, and Details still expands. Never hide, shrink, or grey out the card.
How it generalizes to any finding
Every finding reduces to a subject count over a basis (n of N) plus an optional reference. Pick one of three visual grammars: a proportion bar when the story is how much against a benchmark, unit dots in feed order when the story is where or how often, a segmented bar when the story is a mix of 2 to 4 parts. One visual per card, subject ink alternates blue and green down the stack, everything else stays fixed. Zero results always use the empty-unit treatment above.
