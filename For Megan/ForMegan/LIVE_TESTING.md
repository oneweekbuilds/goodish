### 📱 How to Test the App

#### Option 1: Web Browser (Easiest)
1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: `http://localhost:3000`  
3. The app will load in your browser with full functionality!

#### Option 2: Mobile Device (Best Experience)
1. Download **Expo Go** app from App Store or Google Play
2. Make sure your phone is on the same WiFi network as this computer
3. Open Expo Go and scan the QR code (if displayed in terminal)
4. Or manually enter: `exp://192.168.1.XXX:8081` (replace with your local IP)

#### Option 3: iOS Simulator (Mac Only)
- Press `i` in the terminal where the server is running
- iOS Simulator will open with the app

#### Option 4: Android Emulator 
- Press `a` in the terminal where the server is running  
- Android emulator will open with the app
