# App Store Screenshot Specifications — AlgorithmLens

## Required Sizes
- **iPhone 6.7"** (1290 × 2796): iPhone 15 Pro Max / 16 Pro Max
- **iPhone 6.5"** (1284 × 2778): iPhone 14 Plus / 15 Plus
- **iPhone 5.5"** (1242 × 2208): iPhone 8 Plus (required if supporting older devices)

## Screenshot Plan

### Screen 1: Home Screen — Streak & Feed Score
**What to show:** The home screen with an active streak badge (e.g., "5-day streak") and a feed health score prominently displayed (e.g., 72/100). The daily tip card should be visible below the score.

**App state:**
- User is logged in with at least 5 previous scans
- Streak badge is lit up (orange glow, "5" displayed)
- Feed score shows 72 in the large score ring
- Daily tip card visible below with a compelling insight
- Light mode

**Caption overlay:** "Your feed at a glance"

---

### Screen 2: Broadcast Recording in Progress
**What to show:** The scan overlay screen with the recording indicator active. The timer should show approximately 0:18, and the status should indicate "Recording your feed..."

**App state:**
- Broadcast is actively running (recording dot pulsing red)
- Timer displays "0:18"
- Status text: "Scroll through your feed naturally"
- The background should show a subtle blur suggesting a social media app behind it
- Platform indicator showing "Instagram" selected
- Light mode

**Caption overlay:** "Scan any social media feed"

---

### Screen 3: Analysis Results Summary
**What to show:** The post-scan results summary screen that appears after a broadcast completes analysis. Show headline metrics: total posts analyzed, ad percentage, suggested vs. followed ratio, and a prominent feed score.

**App state:**
- Analysis just completed (success state)
- Shows: "47 posts analyzed"
- Key metrics displayed:
  - Feed Score: 68
  - Ads: 12%
  - Suggested: 58%
  - Political: 4%
- "View Full Dashboard" button visible at bottom
- Light mode

**Caption overlay:** "Instant insights from every scan"

### Screen 4: Dashboard — Overview Tab
**What to show:** The full dashboard with the Overview tab selected. The headline insight should be visible at top (e.g., "58% of your feed was content you didn't choose to follow"). Show the feed composition donut chart and category breakdown below.

**App state:**
- Dashboard loaded with a completed scan's data
- Overview tab active (blue indicator)
- Headline insight text visible at top
- Donut chart showing feed composition (Followed, Suggested, Ads, Other)
- Category breakdown cards visible below chart
- Tab bar at bottom with all 6 tabs visible
- Light mode

**Caption overlay:** "See what your algorithm is really showing you"

---

### Screen 5: History with Comparison Mode
**What to show:** The history screen with two scans selected for comparison. Show side-by-side metric cards comparing two different dates, highlighting changes (e.g., suggested content went from 62% to 51% after the user adjusted their feed).

**App state:**
- History list showing 5+ past scans with dates
- Two scans selected (highlighted with checkmarks)
- Comparison view active at top showing:
  - Scan A: Feb 10 — Suggested: 62%, Ads: 15%
  - Scan B: Feb 17 — Suggested: 51%, Ads: 9%
  - Delta indicators showing improvement (green arrows down)
- Light mode

**Caption overlay:** "Track how your feed changes over time"

## General Guidelines

**Text overlays:** Each screenshot should have a short, compelling caption overlaid at the top or bottom. Use white text on a semi-transparent dark gradient for readability. Font: SF Pro Display, 48pt bold.

**Device frames:** Use iPhone 15 Pro device frames (available from Apple's Design Resources) around each screenshot.

**Consistency:** All screenshots should use light mode to present a clean, professional appearance. Ensure consistent data across screenshots where the same scan might appear (e.g., the results summary metrics should match the dashboard overview if they reference the same scan).

**Data realism:** Use realistic but flattering data. The feed score should be moderate (60–75 range) to feel authentic, not perfect. Ad percentages should be realistic (8–15%). Suggested content should be notably high (50–65%) to demonstrate the app's value proposition — most users are surprised by this number.

**No personal data:** Ensure no real usernames, profile photos, or identifiable content appears in any screenshot. Use generic placeholder data where social media content would be visible.
