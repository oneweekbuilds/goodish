# AlgorithmLens Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 22, 2026
**Effective Date:** February 22, 2026

AlgorithmLens ("we," "us," or "our") operates the AlgorithmLens mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights regarding your data.

We designed AlgorithmLens to help you understand your social media feeds. Because the App captures and analyzes what's on your screen, we take your privacy seriously and want you to fully understand how your data is handled.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email address** — provided via Google OAuth, Apple Sign-In, or direct email registration.
- **User ID** — a unique identifier generated by our authentication system (Supabase).
- **Authentication provider** — whether you signed in with Google, Apple, or email/password.
- **Subscription status** — whether you are on the Free or Plus tier.
- **AI analysis consent** — whether you have opted in to AI-powered feed analysis (you can change this at any time in Settings).
- **Onboarding status** — whether you have completed the App's initial setup flow.

We do not collect your name, phone number, or physical address.

### 1.2 Screen Recording Data

AlgorithmLens captures frames from your device screen during a scan session. Here is exactly how this works:

- **What is captured:** JPEG screenshots of your device screen, taken approximately once every 2.5 seconds while a scan is active.
- **How it's captured:** On iOS, we use Apple's ReplayKit broadcast extension. On Android, we use the MediaProjection API. Both require your explicit permission through a system-level prompt before any capturing begins.
- **On-device processing:** Each frame is deduplicated using perceptual hashing (frames that look nearly identical are discarded). Text visible on-screen is extracted using on-device OCR (optical character recognition). Frames are compressed to JPEG at 75% quality.
- **Maximum capture:** Up to 200 frames per session, with a 10-minute maximum session duration.
- **Where frames are stored temporarily:** On iOS, in an App Group shared container (`group.com.algorithmlens.broadcast/frames/`). On Android, in the app's internal storage (`broadcast/frames/`). These files exist only on your device.
- **When frames are deleted:** Frames are deleted from your device immediately after they have been processed and sent for analysis. If the app crashes or is force-quit during a scan, leftover frames are cleaned up the next time you start a new scan session.

**Important:** Screen recordings may incidentally capture sensitive content visible on your screen (such as notifications, messages, or other apps running in the background). These images are processed temporarily and deleted immediately after analysis — they are never stored permanently and are not reviewed by any person. However, they are sent to Google's Gemini API for automated analysis (see Section 3). We strongly recommend closing sensitive apps before starting a scan.

### 1.3 Scan Results

After your screen frames are analyzed, we store the results of that analysis:

- **Extracted feed items** — information about individual posts identified in your social media feed, including: creator/account names, post text snippets (up to 2,000 characters per post), content categories (e.g., political, ad/sponsored, entertainment), wellbeing flags, hashtags, and position in feed. This text-based data is stored in our database so you can review your scan history.
- **Scan metadata** — which platform was scanned, when the scan occurred, the scan source type (broadcast or webview), and the number of items found.
- **Aggregated statistics** — category breakdowns, ad percentages, and other summary data derived from your scans.

### 1.4 Usage and Preference Data

We store the following locally on your device:

- **Streak data** — your scanning streak (current streak, longest streak, total scans, last scan date). This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.
- **Platform login states** — which social media platforms you've logged into within the App's built-in browser, cached for up to 30 days. This only records that you logged in (e.g., "Instagram: logged in"), not your credentials.
- **Notification preferences** — whether you've enabled scan reminders and how often. We also store your last scan date locally to calculate when to send the next reminder.
- **Theme preferences** — light or dark mode selection.
- **Notification permission status** — whether we have already asked you for notification permission (to avoid asking repeatedly).

### 1.5 Error and Diagnostic Data

We use Sentry for error tracking. When the App encounters an error, Sentry may collect:

- **Error details** — stack traces, error messages, and the sequence of actions leading to the error.
- **User ID and subscription tier** — to help us identify patterns in errors. We explicitly strip your email address, username, and name from all Sentry reports before they are sent.
- **Device information** — device model, operating system version, and app version.
- **Performance data** — sampled at a 10% rate, including page load times and API response durations.

### 1.6 Payment Information

If you subscribe to AlgorithmLens Plus, payment is processed entirely by Stripe. We never see, collect, or store your credit card number, bank account details, or other financial information. Stripe handles this directly in your device's system browser. We receive only a confirmation of your subscription status from Stripe.

## 3. How Screen Recordings Are Processed

This is the most sensitive part of what AlgorithmLens does, so we want to be very clear:

1. **Capture** — When you start a scan, your device captures screenshots of your screen using the operating system's built-in screen recording APIs (ReplayKit on iOS, MediaProjection on Android). You must grant explicit permission through a system dialog before this happens.

2. **On-device processing** — Frames are compressed, deduplicated, and OCR text is extracted — all on your device.

3. **Sent to Google Gemini for AI analysis** — The compressed frame images (as base64-encoded JPEG data) and extracted OCR text are sent to Google's Gemini 2.0 Flash API over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Gemini analyzes the visual content to identify and categorize individual posts in your social media feed.

4. **Results returned** — Gemini returns structured data (JSON) describing the feed items it identified — post text, creator names, content categories, etc. No images are returned by Gemini.

5. **Frames deleted** — After analysis is complete, the frame image files are deleted from your device. The base64-encoded frame data held in memory is released. Only the structured analysis results (text data, not images) are stored.

6. **Results sent to our backend** — The structured analysis results (not the images) are also sent to our backend server for additional enrichment processing (such as political context and tone analysis for Plus users). The backend stores the enriched results in our Supabase database.

7. **Results saved** — The final analysis results are saved to our database (hosted on Supabase) and linked to your account so you can view your scan history.

**What Google receives:** Google's Gemini API receives the JPEG images of your screen frames and the OCR text extracted from them. Google processes this data according to their own privacy policy. We send this data solely for the purpose of AI analysis and do not control how Google retains or processes it after it reaches their servers. See [Google's Gemini API Privacy Policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) for details.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. Each receives certain data as described below:

### 4.1 Google Gemini API (AI Analysis)

- **What we send:** Base64-encoded JPEG screenshots of your screen and OCR-extracted text.
- **Why:** To analyze social media feed content and categorize posts.
- **Their privacy policy:** [Google Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 4.2 Supabase (Authentication and Database)

- **What is stored:** Your account information (email, user ID, auth provider), scan results, and subscription status.
- **Why:** To authenticate you, store your scan history, and manage your profile.
- **Where:** Supabase hosts data on cloud infrastructure. Your data is stored in a Supabase-managed PostgreSQL database with row-level security, meaning each user can only access their own data.
- **Their privacy policy:** [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

### 4.3 Stripe (Payment Processing)

- **What they receive:** Your payment information (entered directly on Stripe's checkout page in your system browser), your subscription plan selection, and a reference to your user account.
- **Why:** To process Plus subscription payments.
- **What we receive back:** Only your subscription status (active, canceled, trial, etc.). We never receive your payment card details.
- **Their privacy policy:** [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy)

### 4.4 Sentry (Error Monitoring)

- **What they receive:** Error reports including stack traces, your user ID, subscription tier, device model, OS version, and app version. Your email and name are explicitly removed before reports are sent.
- **Why:** To identify and fix bugs and crashes.
- **Their privacy policy:** [Sentry Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

### 4.5 Apple and Google (OAuth Authentication)

- **What they facilitate:** When you sign in with Google or Apple, the respective provider handles the authentication flow and shares your email address with us (and, for Apple Sign-In, a relay email if you choose to hide your real email).
- **Why:** To create and authenticate your account.
- **Their privacy policies:** [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/) | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 5. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Screen recording frames (images) | Deleted from your device immediately after analysis completes. If the app crashes, cleaned up on next session start. |
| In-memory frame data (base64) | Released from memory within 5 minutes of analysis completion or when you leave the results screen. |
| Scan results (text data) | Stored on our servers for as long as your account exists, or until you request deletion. |
| Account information | Retained for as long as your account is active. Deleted upon account deletion request. |
| Streak and preference data | Stored locally on your device. Deleted when you uninstall the App or clear app data. |
| Platform login state cache | Automatically expires after 30 days. Stored locally on your device only. |
| Error and diagnostic data | Retained by Sentry for up to 90 days per their default retention policy. |
| Payment records | Retained by Stripe per their data retention policies and applicable financial regulations. |

## 6. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between the App and our servers, and between the App and third-party services, is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
- **Secure credential storage:** Your authentication tokens are stored using your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences via expo-secure-store).
- **Row-level security:** Our Supabase database uses row-level security policies, meaning authenticated users can only access their own data.
- **Minimal data collection:** We collect only the data necessary to provide the App's functionality. Screen frames are processed and deleted — we do not retain the images.
- **PII scrubbing:** Error reports sent to Sentry have personally identifiable information (email, name) stripped before transmission.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

### 7.1 Access and Export Your Data

You can view all your scan history and results within the App. To request a full export of your data in a machine-readable format, contact us at privacy@algorithmlens.com.

### 7.2 Delete Your Data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by:

- Contacting us at privacy@algorithmlens.com
- Using the account deletion option in the App's settings (when available)

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will delete your account, scan results, and profile data from our Supabase database within 30 days. Note that:

- Data already sent to third-party services (Google Gemini, Sentry) is subject to those services' own retention and deletion policies.
- Locally stored data (streaks, preferences) is deleted when you uninstall the App.
- We may retain certain data as required by law (e.g., financial transaction records).

### 7.3 Disable AI Analysis

You can disable AI-powered analysis at any time in the App's Settings by toggling off the AI analysis consent. When disabled, your screen frames will not be sent to Google Gemini for analysis. You can re-enable it at any time.

### 7.4 Withdraw Consent for Screen Recording

You can stop screen recording at any time by ending the broadcast session from your device's Control Center (iOS) or notification shade (Android). You can also revoke the screen recording permission entirely in your device's system settings.

### 7.5 Opt Out of Error Tracking

Currently, Sentry error tracking is active for all users to help us maintain app stability. Error reports are anonymized (email and name removed). If you wish to opt out entirely, contact us at privacy@algorithmlens.com.

### 7.6 Manage Notifications

You can enable or disable scan reminder notifications within the App's settings or through your device's notification settings at any time.

### 7.7 Rights for European Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to: access your personal data, rectify inaccurate data, request erasure of your data, restrict processing of your data, data portability, and object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@algorithmlens.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Our legal bases for processing your data are: consent (for screen recording and AI analysis), contract performance (for providing the service you signed up for), and legitimate interest (for error monitoring and service improvement).

### 7.8 Rights for California Users (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your personal information, and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
