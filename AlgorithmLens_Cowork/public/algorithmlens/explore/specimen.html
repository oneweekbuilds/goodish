What this view can tell you

The blue filled marks carried an ad label. Neutral filled marks had no ad label recorded. Hollow marks mean the ad classification is unknown. A mark is a post, not a minute of attention.

These counts describe only the loaded posts. They cannot explain why a platform selected them. Account names describe appearances, not influence. Origin labels can be inferred; a selected post states the basis supplied by the scan.

Four sheets show four specific scans, not a complete history. Counts alone do not establish a change or a stable rate.
