The honest limit

Honest about limits.

We show what appeared in a sample and distinguish observed labels from inferences. Political content describes a post, not your beliefs. Tone describes a reading of the content, not your mood. A suggested post does not tell us which action led to the recommendation.

This page is an example, not a live scan or a demonstration of a running classifier. It does not record your screen or analyze your feed.
