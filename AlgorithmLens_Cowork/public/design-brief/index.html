Brand inventory BRAND_INVENTORY.md

Brand inventory of the old AlgorithmLens site

Date: 29 September 2026. Source: the Vite/React app as it stood at commit b9a1e192 (the parent of 80175e24, the first commit of the web audit run), checked out as a worktree, installed and run locally. Screenshots at 390 and 1440 are in docs/web-audit/old/ (86 files, headed Chromium through Playwright). Computed-style tallies from the running page are in docs/web-audit/old/old-site-facts.json. Source paths below are relative to AlgorithmLens_Cowork/ at that commit; line numbers are from that commit.

Two pages existed at the same time and both matter:

  • The Coming Soon gate is what the public saw at algorithmlens.com (the flag fails closed, src/config/comingSoon.js:17). It showed the old raster wordmark, the app's navigation with disabled items, a banner, a Beehiiv waitlist form and the app footer. Screenshots: docs/web-audit/old/gate-*.jpg.
  • LandingV12 is the approved brand page (src/components/landing/LandingV12.jsx, a port of "AlgorithmLens Landing v12"), served at / only when the flag is off. It carries almost all the brand character catalogued here. Screenshots: docs/web-audit/old/landing-*.jpg.

The old app's inner pages (methodology, plus, start, privacy, terms, 404) are catalogued briefly at the end because they share the gate's design system, not the landing's.

The canon this inventory checks against: cream paper (#F5F4F0), sentence case, no uppercase, no em dashes, no decorative gradients, token-only colors (brand blue #1868D8 marks the platform's hand, brand green #20A888 marks the reader's own choices, green never as text), Source Serif 4 headlines, calm motion at 160, 240 and 400 ms, the honesty rails of mobile/CLAUDE.md (observable patterns only, hedged inference, no anthropomorphized algorithm, no derived numbers presented as observed, counts before rates).

Fit labels: fits (lift as is), adapt (keep the character, change the form), conflict (cannot ship as is; the adapted version is proposed and the item is in the table at the end for Justin).
