The Simpsons holds the record for the longest-running American animated program, debuting in 1989.
Homer Simpson's famous "D'oh!" was added to the Oxford English Dictionary in 2001.
The yellow skin color of the characters was chosen by creator Matt Groening to grab channel surfers' attention.
Bart Simpson's name is an anagram of "brat," which was intentionally chosen by Matt Groening.
The town of Springfield was deliberately given a common name because it exists in numerous U.S. states, making it impossible to pin down.
