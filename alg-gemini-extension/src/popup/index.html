AI analysis (optional)

AlgorithmLens can optionally use AI to analyze patterns in your feed — tone, political signals, and more. This is always opt-in, always transparent, and currently in beta with limited accuracy.

What gets sent ›
Post captions and hashtags only. No images, no account info.
What doesn't get sent ›
Your identity, photos, videos, or browsing history.

You can change this anytime from the extension popup. AI analysis sends post text to Google's Gemini AI for processing. Google does not use this data to train AI models.
